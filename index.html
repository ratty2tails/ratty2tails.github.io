The Ratty2Tails Papers

Long-form essays on money, its history, and the machines that might replace it.

Two series live here. Monetary History traces money from the ledger to the gold standard to Bitcoin, mostly through an Austrian, sound-money lens. The Machine Series looks at the technical and computational forces — cryptography chief among them — that could upend all of it again.
